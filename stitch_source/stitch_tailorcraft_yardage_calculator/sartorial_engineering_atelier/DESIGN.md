## Brand & Style

This design system draws its ethos from the cutting rooms of Savile Row and the uncompromising discipline of bespoke garment engineering. It balances timeless artisanal tailoring with the exacting precision of structural blueprints. The visual environment communicates master-level pedigree, archival permanence, and architectural exactitude.

The target audience encompasses master cutters, tailoring apprentices, bespoke clientele, and luxury production directors. The interface evokes quiet authority, tactile craftsmanship, and analytical focus. Interactions must feel measured, intentional, and composed—eschewing frivolous ornamentation in favor of crisp rule lines, structural framing, and typographic distinction.

The stylistic direction blends **Editorial Heritage** with **Technical Precision**:
- High-contrast editorial display serifs denote heritage, commission provenance, and bespoke tiers.
- Pragmatic, low-contrast neutral grotesques ground the interface in technical measurements, order states, and pattern drafting tables.
- Subtle tailor-inspired motifs—such as basting-stitch dividers, millimeter tick margins, and brass hardware highlights—provide domain resonance without cluttering operational workflows.

## Colors

The color palette reflects the tangible materials of the cutting suite: English wool suiting, chalk lines, measuring tapes, and aged pattern parchment.

### Color Roles & Semantics
- **Primary (`#121721` — Midnight Wool):** The foundational ink and deepest canvas tone. Represents superfine worsted wool and rich archival cloth. Applied to structural headers, primary actions, and high-emphasis boundaries.
- **Secondary (`#C89B3C` — Aged Brass):** The accent of tailor's shears, antique measuring ferrules, and hallmark insignias. Applied sparingly to active states, selected garment phases, badge borders, and primary focal highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#64748B` — Tailor's Slate / Chalk):** Evokes marking chalk and slate drafting tables. Applied to secondary labels, measurement unit indicators, basting lines, and inactive structure.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5` — Ecru Parchment):** The master ground tone. A warm, non-glare ecru that mirrors unbleached cotton canvas and bespoke pattern paper.
- **Surface Elevation Steps:**
  - `surface-base`: `#FBF9F5` (Ecru Parchment)
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp Linen White)
  - `surface-elevated`: `#F4EFE6` (Drafting Board Warm Stone)
  - `surface-inset`: `#EDE6D9` (Pressed Pattern Felt)
- **Functional & Technical Accents:**
  - `border-fine`: `#E3DCD1` (Pressed Cloth Edge)
  - `border-chalk`: `#CBD5E1` (Basting Mark Line)
  - `status-cut`: `#1E293B` (Pattern Locked)
  - `status-basting`: `#C89B3C` (In Fitting)
  - `status-finished`: `#2D5A43` (Master Press Complete)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy separates narrative atelier prestige from shop-floor measurement utility:

- **Display & Headings (Playfair Display):** Used for client names, garment categories, collection titles, and editorial narratives. High thick-to-thin stroke modulation brings editorial confidence without sacrificing legibility at structural sizes.
- **Data, Measures & Interaction (DM Sans):** Selected for its clear geometric structure and open counters. It provides total legibility when reading chest, waist, yoke, and sleeve measurements on mobile tablets in variable atelier light.
- **Tabular Figures & Numeric Precision:** In all measurement matrices, cut sheets, and client profile data points, apply CSS `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to DM Sans to ensure vertical alignment across dimension lists.
- **Technical Badges:** `label-technical` is rendered in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.08em`) to mimic stamped bespoke garment tags and ticket pocket identification.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors a bespoke cutting table: calibrated, structured, and rhythmically aligned.

### Grid Construction
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column structured grid. Outer margins scale to `margin-desktop` (3rem), gutters set to `gutter-lg` (2rem). Content width caps at a maximum master canvas of 1440px to retain garment blueprint proportions.
- **Tablet / Fitting Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `gutter` (1.5rem) and `margin` (2rem). Designed for two-handed tablet use during live fittings.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column compact grid with `gutter-sm` (0.75rem) and `margin-mobile` (1rem).

### Layout Rules
- **Proportional Discipline:** Section margins align with standard tailoring scale multiples (8px base cadence).
- **Split Blueprints:** Cutting sheets and measurement logs must use a dual-pane asymmetric split: 4 columns for garment specifications, 8 columns for master measurement input and draft preview.
- **Padding Insets:** Atelier cards and measurement sheets utilize asymmetric padding—slightly taller vertical breathing room (`space-lg`) to balance the horizontal precision lines (`space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating, heavy drop shadows in favor of **Crisp Architectural Layering** and **Subtle Parchment Stacking**. Depth is established through physical framing rather than artificial blur.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Cutting Mat / Base Canvas):** Ground parchment `#FBF9F5` with optional faint structural hairline guide grids (0.5px `#E3DCD1`).
2. **Level 1 (Card & Docket Panels):** Clean linen `#FFFFFF` with a 1px solid hairline border in `#E3DCD1`. Grounded flat, no shadow.
3. **Level 2 (Active Pattern / Active Fitting Sheet):** Raised linen sheet with a crisp double-edge visual: a 1px border in `#C89B3C` combined with a soft, natural ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(18, 23, 33, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(18, 23, 33, 0.02)`.
4. **Level 3 (Inspection Modals & Drape Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` framed by a 1px `#121721` border, backed by a warm tinted scrim: `rgba(18, 23, 33, 0.45)` with a 3px backdrop blur to isolate fitting focus.

### Stitch & Line Details
To convey tailoring pedigree without ornamental kitsch:
- Dividers between measurement groups utilize a precise "basting line" pattern: `border-top: 1px dashed #CBD5E1` with a dash pattern of `4px 4px`.
- Section terminators use a double hairline: two 1px solid lines spaced by 2px in `#E3DCD1`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Sharp (`0`)** profile. Every element honors the sharp edge of the tailor's shears, the steel ruler, and the folded textile.

- **Corner Radius:** 0px across all containers, inputs, buttons, cards, and modal sheets. 
- **Structural Integrity:** Crisp right angles preserve the technical blueprint aesthetic, differentiating the interface from generic rounded SaaS tools.
- **Notches & Chamfers:** Minor technical markers (such as size pills and fitting badges) may feature a 45-degree corner clip (2px chamfer) on top-right edges to mimic traditional bespoke card tags.
- **Hairlines:** Interactive and structural boundaries use absolute 1px lines. Avoid rounded pills, capsule tabs, or circular avatars—use square aspect ratios and crisp bounding boxes throughout.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Commission / Execute Action):** Solid `#121721` background, `#FBF9F5` typography, 0px border radius. Padding: `12px 24px`. Subtle transition to `#1A202C` on hover. Focus state defined by a 1px offset brass outline (`#C89B3C`).
- **Secondary (Technical Alteration):** Parchment `#FBF9F5` background, `#121721` text, 1px solid `#121721` border. On hover: backgrounds invert to `#121721` and text to `#FBF9F5`.
- **Accent (Master Measure):** `#C89B3C` background with `#121721` typography for fitting approvals and final lock actions.
- **Ghost (Draft / Cancel):** Transparent ground, `#64748B` typography, with a 1px dashed underline on hover.

### Inputs & Measurement Fields
- **Measurement Matrix Input:** Sharp rectangle, `#FFFFFF` ground, 1px solid `#CBD5E1` border. DM Sans bold numeric text (`15px`). 
- **Unit Affix:** Integrated right-hand unit label (`"in"` or `"cm"`) rendered in `label-technical` (`#64748B`), vertically separated by a 1px hairline divider.
- **Active State:** Border transitions to 1px solid `#C89B3C` with an interior corner notch indicator.
- **Tolerance Warning:** If a measurement exceeds anatomical balance rules, the border shifts to 1px `#B91C1C` with an inline annotation badge.

### Chips & Basting Markers
- **Fabric Swatch Chips:** Square aspect ratio (32x32px), bordered with 1px `#E3DCD1`. Active selection bounded by an inset 2px `#C89B3C` border.
- **Stage Badges (e.g., "CANVAS BASING", "SECOND FITTING", "CUTTING"):** Strict uppercase `label-technical`. Background: `#F4EFE6`, text: `#121721`, border: 1px solid `#E3DCD1`. Padding: `4px 8px`.

### Lists & Measurement Logs
- **Anatomical Data Rows:** Alternating row tone between `#FBF9F5` and `#FFFFFF`.
- **Row Dividers:** Hairline dashed border (`1px dashed #E3DCD1`).
- **Data Columns:** Measure name left-aligned in `DM Sans 14px`, numerical value right-aligned in tabular `DM Sans 15px 700`, followed by variance indicators (+/- 0.25) in `#64748B`.

### Atelier Cards (Garment Dossier)
- **Structure:** Linen white `#FFFFFF` surface encased in 1px solid `#E3DCD1`.
- **Card Header:** Playfair Display `headline-sm` with a secondary `label-caption` commission number (e.g., `REF #SR-1849-04`) placed top-right.
- **Footer Section:** Bounded by a 1px dashed basting divider. Contains cutter signature badge, cloth mill stamp (e.g., "Dormeuil 15.5oz"), and delivery phase.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Exact 16x16px square, 0px radius, 1px solid `#121721` border. Checked state displays a solid inner square fill (10x10px) in `#121721` or `#C89B3C`.
- **Radio Buttons:** 16x16px diamond orientation or sharp square with an inset center dot. No circular pill shapes.

### Specialized Component: The Cutter's Blueprint Bar
- A persistent bottom or lateral utility strip during live fittings. Displays real-time anatomical balance differentials (Front Length vs. Back Balance, Inseam Drop) with high-contrast brass readouts (`#C89B3C`) on Midnight Wool (`#121721`), ensuring rapid legibility when moving around the client.
